Frequently asked questions

Is it free for travelers?

Yes. Sending requests and comparing offers is completely free — you only pay for the trip you choose.

How do I know a partner is trustworthy?

We only activate partners after verifying their license, and every offer shows the partner's rating.

How long do I receive offers?

You choose when sending the request: 24, 48 or 72 hours.

Can I talk to a partner before booking?

Of course — there's an in-app chat with every partner who sends you an offer.

How do booking and payment work?

Once you accept an offer, the partner contacts you directly to confirm the booking and payment.
