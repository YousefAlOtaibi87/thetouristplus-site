Frequently asked questions

Is it free for travelers?

Yes. Posting a request and comparing offers is completely free — you only pay for the trip you choose.

How do I know an agency is trustworthy?

No agency is activated until we verify its Ministry of Commerce license, and you see past travelers' ratings on every offer.

How long do offers stay open?

You choose when you post: 24, 48 or 72 hours.

Can I talk to the agency before booking?

Yes — there's in-app chat with every agency that sends you an offer.

How do I pay?

With KNET or credit card, inside the platform.
